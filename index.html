The roster

Fifteen beasts. Five elements.

Six starters answer your call from the first match. Every two ranked wins earn an egg that hatches one of the rest.

How to play

Three picks. One fight. No second chances.

I

Build your squad

Choose three monsters for the Front, Middle and Back. Guards soak hits, Strikers deal damage, Mystics heal and shield.

II

Counter their elements

See the opponent before you fight. Each element hits the next one for +30%, so reorder your team to win the matchups.

III

Climb the season

Win to gain RP and level up, from Bronze to Mythic. Your last team defends your rank while you're away.

Season prize pool

The top three take the pool

A share of $FANG's trading fees flows into each season's pool. When the 14 days are up, the final standings freeze and the top three are paid on-chain, in public.

Fair play

Skill decides, not the browser

The server decides every match

Battles run on the game server the moment you lock in. Your browser only plays them back, so no one can edit a result.

No rerolls

The battle seed stays secret until you press Fight, and a found opponent waits until you face them.

Real opponents

Ranked players face other players' defense teams near their rating. Bots fill in when no one is close.

Sign in, never sign away

Connect a Solana wallet by signing one message. It is not a transaction and can't move funds.

The arena is waiting

Free to start. No wallet needed until you want to rank.
